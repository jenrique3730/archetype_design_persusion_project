---

## 1. Chosen Archetype
* **Archetype:** The Sage (with elements of The Ruler)
* **Core Motivation:** Seeking clarity, analyzing structural frameworks, objective problem-solving, and establishing systematic order.

---

## 2. AI Recommendation & Reflection
* **Why AI Suggested It:** The AI highlighted a focus on methodical organization, setting up technical workflows, evaluating facts critically, and leading projects by providing clear architectural guidance rather than raw emotional hype.
* **Do I Agree?** Yes. As the project lead and owner of Modernist Design, I naturally gravitate toward structured systems, clarity, grid alignment, and objective functionality over clutter.

---

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual Strategy
* **Imagery:** Minimalist architectural photography, clean drafting grids, organized workstations, and structural geometric forms.
* **Color Palette:**
  * Primary: Deep Navy / Slate (`#0F172A`)
  * Neutral: Off-White / Paper (`#F8FAFC`)
  * Accent: International Klein Blue (`#002FA7`) or Bauhaus Yellow (`#FACC15`)
* **Typography:** Clean, objective sans-serif fonts such as Helvetica, Inter, or Univers. Monospace accents for technical precision.
* **Sample Phrasing:**
  * *"Structure enables creativity."*
  * *"Clarity, precision, and purpose in execution."*

---

## 4. Applying Cialdini’s Persuasion Principles to Personal Brand
* **Authority:** Establishing technical credibility by maintaining strict Git standards, producing well-researched design histories, and citing primary historical sources.
* **Commitment & Consistency:** Following through on agreed milestone dates, maintaining transparent issue trackers, and holding team deliverables to standard rubrics.
* **Social Proof:** Showcasing collaborative repository metrics, clear peer-reviewed pull requests, and verified contributions.
